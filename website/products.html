ContentForge

From an idea to a video, using tools on your own machine.

A local-first, AI-assisted short-form video workbench. ContentForge brings research, script drafting, narration, scene planning, and video rendering into one workflow, with local models and locally stored media.

  • Draft scripts with a local Ollama model
  • Generate narration with Piper and render with FFmpeg
  • Build short-form video with local ComfyUI / Wan models
  • Review and edit before rendering or publishing

ContentForge is an active experimental project, not a production-ready publishing service. Windows is the supported quick-start platform.

Sysfix

Understand what's wrong before you start changing things.

An AI-assisted Linux diagnostics and repair tool. Sysfix checks common system and hardware issues, explains what it finds, and helps you work through fixes without making changes behind your back.

  • Checks memory, storage, temperatures, audio, and system details
  • Optional local AI analysis through Ollama
  • Fast Sweep and interactive Deep Dive troubleshooting
  • Shows proposed actions and asks for confirmation before applying them
