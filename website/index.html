The engine

Everything below is running in the real engine. These pictures are straight out of the Player, no touch-ups.

Blow it all up

Explosions send a shockwave racing outwards that breaks joints, flings loose parts and knocks players over.

  • Fireballs that roll up into smoke and mushroom clouds
  • Happy-Wheels-style ragdolls: limbs come off, oil and bolts fly (you can turn gore down or off)
  • Bombs in water leave a crater and a column of spray
  • One line of Lua: Explode(position, radius, power)

Particles and fire NEW

Drop a ParticleEmitter, Fire, Smoke or Sparkles into any part and it starts spraying.

  • Rate, colour and size over time, speed, spread, gravity and drag
  • Glowing particles with LightEmission
  • Emit(50) for a one-off burst
  • Plus Trails behind swords, Beams between two points, and Highlights that outline anything

Light it up NEW

Set the time of day, the sun, the fog and the clouds, then finish the look with screen effects.

  • Bloom, blur, colour grading, sun rays and depth of field
  • Your own Sky with stars, sun and moon
  • Point lights, spot lights and glowing Neon
  • Real shadows and reflections

Terrain and water

Generate rolling hills in one click, then Raise, Lower, Smooth, Flatten and Paint them with brushes.

  • Grass, dirt, sand, rock, snow and mud
  • Characters walk up slopes and loose parts roll down them
  • Water with waves that pushes and floats things, so boats work
  • Scripts can sculpt it too: workspace.Terrain:FillBall(...)

Real physics, 18 materials

Every material has its own look, weight, grip and bounce. Ice is slippery, Brick is grippy, Metal is heavy.

  • Ropes, springs, hinges, motors, welds, ball sockets and sliders
  • Classic BodyMovers like BodyVelocity and BodyGyro
  • Collision groups so team doors let only one team through
  • Union, Negate and Intersect to carve your own shapes

Lua scripting, the way you already know it

-- A door that opens when you walk up and press E.
local door = script.Parent
local prompt = Instance.new("ProximityPrompt")
prompt.ActionText = "Open"
prompt.Parent = door

prompt.Triggered:Connect(function(player)
    door.Transparency = 0.8
    door.CanCollide = false
    task.wait(3)
    door.Transparency = 0
    door.CanCollide = true
end)

-- And when things get boring:
Explode(door.Position, 12, 2)

If you've scripted on Roblox, you already know how. Guts&Bolts uses the same Luau language and the same names: Instance.new, Touched, leaderstats, Humanoid, TweenService and the rest.

  • Type checking, +=, continue and {name} strings
  • A script editor with autocomplete, line numbers and the error line marked
  • A Developer Console (F9) in every game
  • require models straight from the Library
  • Game passes and developer products to sell in your game

Make your own with Studio

Studio comes in the same download. It looks and works like the Studio you know: Explorer, Properties, Toolbox and a ribbon of tools.

  • Press Play to test straight away, or test with 2, 3 or 4 players on your own computer
  • Build with friends in Team Create
  • Terrain tools, an Animation Editor and Modeling mode
  • Ready-made pieces: spawns, checkpoints, jump pads, bombs, a nuke
  • Open and save Roblox .rbxl and .rbxm files, hats included
  • Plugins, and a Library full of models, decals and sounds
  • Publish in one click and everyone can play it

An AI helper that knows Studio

The Assistant tab can build, fix and explain things for you. Ask "why won't my barrel fall?" and it looks.

You can also connect outside AI apps (like Claude Desktop or Claude Code) over MCP. They can see your game, add and change objects, write scripts, run playtests and roll back if something goes wrong.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The apps, Studio and the online platform are free. Bolts (the money in the catalog) are earned by playing.

Who is it for?

Adults (18+). Expect strong language, crude humour and cartoon gore. Gore can be turned down or off in the settings.

Can I bring my Roblox builds?

Yes. Studio opens and saves .rbxl and .rbxm places and models, and Roblox hats come in with their real mesh and texture. Scripts use the same names, so most of them only need small changes.

Do I need to keep updating it?

No. The Player and Studio update themselves when you open them.

Is it made by Roblox?

No. Guts&Bolts is a free, independent hobby project and isn't connected to Roblox Corporation.
