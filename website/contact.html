Guts&Bolts is an independent, free hobby platform for games, communities and more, for adults (18+). It's made and run by a small team of staff and volunteers. Every message reaches real people.

The quickest way: a support ticket

Open a ticket on the Support page. You don't need to be logged in. Staff answer there, and if you leave an email address we'll tell you when they do. Pick the topic that fits:

You want to...Pick
Get back into your accountI forgot my password
See, fix, delete or get a copy of your data, or opt out of somethingMy data
Report your work uploaded without permission (or send a counter-notice)Copyright (see the Copyright Policy)
Tell us an account belongs to someone under 18 (parents start here)Someone under 18
Anything else, including press, partnerships and advertisersSomething else

Report a player, game or post

Use the Report button on the thing itself (a profile, game, item, message, group, comment or post). Reports go straight to the moderators, who can see what was reported. If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Security problems

Found a security bug? Please report it privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting, not in public. Our policy is in SECURITY.md, and security.txt has the same details for tools.

Community chat

For chatting with other players (not official support), join us on Lyreto.
