Copyright Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026. This goes with our Terms of Service (section 6).

Players upload games, models, clothing, pictures and sounds to Guts&Bolts. We respect other people's work, and we expect players to as well. If something here is yours and was uploaded without your permission, tell us and we'll take it down. This page follows the notice-and-takedown process of the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), and we handle notices from other countries the same way.

1. Report something that copies your work

Send a notice on the Support page (pick "Copyright: someone uploaded my work"). You don't need an account. Please include:

  1. What your work is (a description, or a link to where it's published).
  2. Where the copy is on Guts&Bolts: the link to the game, item, picture or post.
  3. Your name and how to reach you (an email address).
  4. This sentence: "I have a good faith belief that the use described above is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law."
  5. This sentence: "The information in this notice is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, I am the owner, or authorized to act on behalf of the owner, of the copyright that is allegedly infringed."
  6. Your full name typed as your signature.

Only send a notice for something you own (or are allowed to act for). Under US law, someone who knowingly sends a false notice can have to pay for the harm it causes.

2. What happens next

  • Staff check the notice. If it has what's needed, we remove the upload or block access to it, usually within a few days.
  • We tell the person who uploaded it that it was taken down because of a copyright notice, and pass on the notice (we may leave out your contact details if you ask, unless the law says we can't).
  • Bolts the uploader earned from it can be taken back.

3. If your upload was taken down by mistake (counter-notice)

If you think your upload was removed by mistake, or you had permission, send a counter-notice on the Support page while logged in, with:

  1. What was removed and where it was (the link or its name).
  2. This sentence: "I swear, under penalty of perjury, that I have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of a mistake or misidentification of the material to be removed or disabled."
  3. Your name, address and phone number, and this sentence: "I consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district in which my address is located (or, if outside the United States, any judicial district in which Guts&Bolts may be found), and I will accept service of process from the person who provided the original notice or their agent."
  4. Your full name typed as your signature.

We pass the counter-notice to the person who sent the first notice. If they don't tell us within 10 business days that they've gone to court about it, we can put the upload back 10 to 14 business days after we got your counter-notice.

4. Repeat infringers

Accounts that keep uploading other people's work get banned. As a rule, a third valid copyright notice against the same account (or one serious case, like re-uploading something we already removed) means the account is closed.

5. Trademarks and other problems

For trademarks (like someone using your brand's name or logo), impersonation, or anything else that isn't copyright, use the same Support page and pick "Something else", or report the item from its page.
