Cookie Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026. This goes with our Privacy Policy.

A cookie is a small file a website saves in your browser. Websites can also save things in "local storage", which works the same way. This page lists everything Guts&Bolts saves in your browser, and what the companies we work with may save, so you know exactly what's there and why.

2. Advertising (Google AdSense)

Banner ads show on a few pages (Home, Games, Catalog and Updates), never to Boltworks Club members. When an ad loads, Google may set or read its own cookies. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland this only happens if you said yes. Everywhere else you can say no to personalized ads with Privacy choices, or by turning on Global Privacy Control in your browser.

Google decides the exact names and times, and can change them. Their full, current list is at policies.google.com/technologies/cookies, and how Google uses data from sites like ours is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Payments

If you open the checkout page to pay for something, Stripe loads its payment form and may set cookies to stop card fraud. That only happens on the checkout page. See Stripe's cookie policy.

4. What we don't use

  • No analytics or tracking tools (no Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, no heatmaps).
  • No cookies from us that follow you to other websites.

5. How to change or remove them

  • Your choice about ads: the Privacy choices link at the bottom of every page. You can change your mind any time.
  • Global Privacy Control: if your browser or an extension sends it, we treat it as "no" to personalized ads, without you clicking anything.
  • Your browser: you can see and delete cookies and site data in your browser's settings. Deleting the "needed" ones logs you out and resets your display choices.
  • Google's own settings: adssettings.google.com turns off personalized ads from Google everywhere, and aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.eu cover other ad companies.

6. Changes

If we add or remove a cookie, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
