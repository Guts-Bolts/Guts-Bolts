What it does

Start with a quick health check, then look closer at whatever needs it.

1

Health check

CPU, memory, swap, disks, the busiest programs and temperatures (when your PC reports them).

2

Find problems

Checks hardware, sound, network and settings, then sorts what it finds into problems, warnings and info.

3

Speed

Finds programs eating your memory or disk, and warns early when a disk starts to wear out.

4

Security

Open ports, firewall, failed logins and odd-looking programs, with optional virus scanning.

5

Network

Checks your connection, DNS and how fast your router answers.

6

Two ways to use it

A desktop app with tabs and charts, or quick commands in a terminal (handy over SSH).

How to start

You need a modern Linux and Python 3.10 or newer. The ready-made package is the easiest way.

  1. Download the package for your Linux

    RPM for Fedora, RHEL and openSUSE. DEB for Debian, Ubuntu and Mint.

    Go to downloads ↓

  2. Install it

    Open a terminal in the folder you downloaded it to and run the line for your Linux:

    sudo dnf install ./sysfix-ai-1.2.0-1.fc44.noarch.rpm
    sudo apt install ./sysfix-ai_1.2.0-1_all.deb

  3. Open it

    Find Sysfix in your apps menu, or type one of these:

    sysfix
    sysfix health

Commands to try

Type sysfix --help to see every command in your version.

sysfix health
A quick health report. Changes nothing, doesn't need root.
sysfix health --json
The same report as JSON, for scripts.
sysfix check
The full check for problems.
sysfix security
Look at security settings.
sysfix network
Check your connection.
sysfix terminal
The menu version for the terminal.

Fyxa can help

Fyxa is the Guts&Bolts AI helper. In Sysfix it explains what the checks found and helps you dig into logs, in plain words or in tech-speak.

  • Runs on your own PC with Ollama. Nothing is sent away.
  • Remembers your hardware and past fixes, on your PC.
  • Optional: Sysfix works fine without it.

Is it safe?

Sysfix looks first and only changes things when you say so.

  • The health check only reads. It doesn't need root and changes nothing.
  • Fixes wait for you. Automatic repairs are switched off in this version.
  • Some extras need root or extra packages, and are still experimental.

Questions

Does it work on Windows or Mac?

No, Sysfix is for Linux only.

Do I need AI to use it?

No. All the checks work without it. Fyxa is an extra that needs Ollama and a model.

Will it change my settings by itself?

No. Automatic repairs are off in this version. Read what it suggests before you run anything that needs root.

Why does something say "unavailable"?

Some checks need a sensor, a tool, root or a kernel feature your PC doesn't have. "Unavailable" doesn't mean that part passed.

Where do I report a bug?

On the Guts&Bolts help page. Tip: paste the output of sysfix health into G&B Paste and share the link.
