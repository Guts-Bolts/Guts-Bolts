How it works

Five steps, and you can check and change everything in between.

1

Pick a topic

Type one in, or let it collect ideas from news feeds you choose.

2

Get a script

A local AI (Ollama) writes the narration, a hook, captions, hashtags and notes on what to fact-check.

3

Fix it up

Read it, change anything you like, and mark it as checked.

4

Make the video

Piper reads it out, the scenes get made, and captions, music and sound effects line up with the voice.

5

Post it yourself

You get an MP4 and a captions file. ContentForge never posts anything for you.

Fyxa, your creative helper

Fyxa lives inside ContentForge too. Ask it for a creative plan, a script or a scene-by-scene storyboard.

  • Everything it writes is a draft until you say it's good
  • Every change keeps the old version, so you can compare
  • Checks finished videos (size, length, sound) and can make a better version from your notes
  • It can look at your project but can't change or post anything by itself

AI video, if your PC can

Without extras, ContentForge makes moving title-card scenes. With a strong NVIDIA card it can make real AI video clips too.

  • Uses ComfyUI and the Wan 2.1 model (about 10 GB download)
  • Works from about 8 GB of video memory. 12 GB or more is better
  • Optional AI sound effects and exact word timing, also on your PC

How to start

  1. Download and unzip

    Get the ZIP below and unzip it into a folder you can write to.

  2. Run the setup

    Open PowerShell in that folder and run:

    Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process Bypass; .\setup.ps1

  3. Get a writing model

    With Ollama running, download a model and put its name in the .env file as OLLAMA_MODEL:

    ollama pull qwen3:4b

  4. Start it

    Double-click start.bat, then open 127.0.0.1:8787 in your browser.

Keep it on your PC. The ContentForge dashboard has no password, so don't open it up to the internet.

Questions

Does it post to YouTube or TikTok?

No. It makes the video and you post it yourself. That way nothing goes out that you haven't watched.

Is it really all on my PC?

Yes. The AI runs on your computer. It only goes online to download its parts during setup, and to read the news feeds you pick.

Can I trust the facts in the script?

Not on their own. The AI can get things wrong, so ContentForge lists what to check. Look things up yourself before you post.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Windows is the one we support. Linux may work if you set the paths up yourself.

Is it finished?

Not yet. It's an early test version: things can break, and some parts (like stock footage or posting) aren't there.

Where do I report a bug?

On the Guts&Bolts help page.
