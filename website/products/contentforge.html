How it works

Five steps, and you can check and change everything in between. You can do one video, or a whole batch at once.

1

Paste your ideas

One question per line, as many as you like, or let it collect ideas from news feeds you choose.

2

Get a script

An AI writes the narration, a hook, captions, hashtags and notes on what to fact-check. Use the free one on your PC (Ollama), or Claude with your own key.

3

Fix it up

Turn on "check before making the video" to read and change each script first. The queue shows where every video is.

4

Make the video

Piper reads it out, the scenes get made, and captions, music and sound effects line up with the voice.

5

Post it

Pick a schedule, like one video every 8 hours. ContentForge uploads to the accounts you connect, or tells you when it's time to post one yourself.

Fyxa, your creative helper

Fyxa lives inside ContentForge too. Ask it for a creative plan, a script or a scene-by-scene storyboard.

  • Everything it writes is a draft until you say it's good
  • Every change keeps the old version, so you can compare
  • Checks finished videos (size, length, sound) and can make a better version from your notes
  • It can look at your project but can't change or post anything by itself

AI video, if your PC can

Without extras, ContentForge makes moving title-card scenes. With a strong NVIDIA card it can make real AI video clips too.

  • Uses ComfyUI and the Wan 2.1 model (about 10 GB download)
  • Works from about 8 GB of video memory. 12 GB or more is better
  • Optional AI sound effects and exact word timing, also on your PC

Only for the extra AI video clips (8 GB+ of video memory)

How to start

  1. Install it

    On Windows, run ContentForge-Setup.exe. It doesn't need admin rights. On a Mac, open the .dmg and drag ContentForge into Applications.

  2. Get past the warning

    The app isn't signed yet, so your computer doesn't know it. On Windows press More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, right-click the app the first time and pick Open.

  3. Press the Setup buttons

    Open the Setup screen and press Install for the voice and Download model for the writer. Everything turns green when it's ready.

  4. Make videos

    Go to Create, paste your ideas, pick a look and a schedule, and press Make videos. Connect your accounts on the Accounts screen to post them.

Your stuff stays yours. ContentForge only talks to your own computer, and your keys only go to the platform they belong to.

Questions

Does it post to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram?

Yes, through each one's official way of uploading, with accounts you connect yourself. YouTube works fully, with scheduling. TikTok sends videos to your drafts until TikTok approves your app. Instagram needs a Business or Creator account. For Facebook, X and anything else, ContentForge tells you when it's time and you post it yourself.

Is it really all on my PC?

The app and your videos are. It goes online to download its parts during setup, find free pictures, read the news feeds you pick, and upload to the accounts you connect. If you pick Claude as the writer, your topics are sent to Claude to write the script.

Can I trust the facts in the script?

Not on their own. The AI can get things wrong, so ContentForge lists what to check. Look things up yourself before you post.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Windows is ready. A Mac (Apple chip) download is on the way and turns on here by itself when it's out. Linux may work from the source code if you set it up yourself.

Is it finished?

Not yet. It's an early test version: things can break, and some parts (like stock footage) aren't there.
