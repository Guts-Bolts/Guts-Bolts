Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026. This covers the website (gutsandbolts.net), Studio, the Player and our game servers.

Guts&Bolts is a free hobby game platform for adults (18+). This page says, in plain words, what we keep about you, why, who else handles it, and how to get it removed.

Our promise: we never sell your data

Guts&Bolts does not sell users' personal data. We do not sell, rent, trade, license, or provide personal data to data brokers or advertisers in exchange for money or anything else of value. We do not sell user data whatsoever. Google AdSense is a separate advertising service: when an ad is displayed, Google's technology may collect or receive information as described in the Ads and cookies section below. That is Google's ad processing, not Guts&Bolts selling your data.

We use information to operate and protect Guts&Bolts, provide Fyxa features you choose to use, and support Google ads where they are displayed. We do not give advertisers access to your account, private messages, uploads, or other platform records for them to market directly to you.

1. What we keep

  • Your account: username, user number, your public signing key (your password never leaves your device in a readable form), avatar, friends, groups, what you own and make, and your Bolts history.
  • An email address, only if you add one. It's used for account emails, like login codes and resetting your password, and for answers to support tickets.
  • What you post and send: forum posts, comments, messages, chats, games and uploads.
  • Safety records: reports, warnings and bans, so staff can keep the platform safe.
  • Technical info: like your IP address and browser, which our host (Cloudflare) uses to keep the site running and to stop attacks and bots. We keep short logs with web addresses cleaned of private parts.
  • Fyxa information: if you use Fyxa, the prompts, conversation content, relevant context and tool information you choose to provide may be processed to answer your requests. If you use Fyxa's account-based conversation or memory features, the related session details and memories are stored for your account.

2. How we use information

We use information for these limited purposes:

  • Running Guts&Bolts: to provide your account, games, purchases, inventory, social features, support, moderation, security, fraud prevention, troubleshooting and platform operations.
  • Fyxa: to respond to prompts and carry out the tasks you request, using the context and information needed for that request. Fyxa account memories are private to your account and can be managed or deleted through the available Fyxa controls.
  • Google ads: Google may process information through its advertising technology to serve, personalize, measure and protect ads, subject to your choices and Google's policies.

We do not currently use or transmit users' uploads to train Fyxa or another AI model. The Fyxa upload preference controls are preparatory controls for possible future use; they do not mean a training pipeline is currently running. If that changes, this policy and the product's notices and controls must be updated before that use begins.

Where the law asks for a reason (like the GDPR in Europe), we rely on providing the service you signed up for, keeping it safe (our legitimate interests), and your consent for ad cookies where required.

3. Fyxa AI

  • Cloud Fyxa: when you choose Guts&Bolts cloud mode, your conversation is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI so it can generate a response. Cloudflare processes that request as the AI infrastructure provider.
  • Local Fyxa: when you run Fyxa on your own computer through Ollama, the conversation is processed on your device and is not sent to Guts&Bolts' cloud Fyxa service.
  • Conversation memory: if you use Fyxa's memory features, saved memories and session information are associated with your account and are not visible to other users through those features. You can delete them using the available controls.
  • Model training: no user uploads are currently selected or transmitted for model training. A setting about possible future use of public uploads does not mean that training is currently taking place.

4. Ads and cookies

Guts&Bolts shows banner ads from Google AdSense on a few pages (like Home, Games and the Catalog). Ads are never shown to Boltworks Club members.

  • When Google ads are loaded, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies and process information such as your IP address, browser or device information, ad interactions and page activity to show ads, measure performance and prevent ad fraud. Depending on your settings and location, ads may be personalized using information about your visits to this and other websites.
  • How Google uses information from sites that show its ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can turn off personalized ads from Google at adssettings.google.com, and learn about opting out of other ad companies at aboutads.info.
  • In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask before any ad cookies are used. If you pick "Only necessary", you won't see ads at all.

Our own site only stores what it needs to work: your login key (encrypted, in your browser), your language, a few display choices, and your cookie answer. Those don't track you anywhere else.

Done. You'll be asked again on the next page with ads.

5. Who else handles your data

We do not sell your data to these services or to anyone else. We use service providers to operate specific parts of Guts&Bolts. They receive or process information as needed for the service they provide:

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and servers, provides security and bot protection, and provides Workers AI for cloud Fyxa requests.
  • Supabase stores some of the site's supporting data (like leaderboards and activity feeds).
  • Brevo sends account emails, if you added an email address.
  • Google AdSense serves ads and processes advertising-related information as described in section 4.
  • Stripe handles payment processing when you make a purchase. Guts&Bolts does not receive or store your full card number.

6. How long we keep it

For as long as your account is open. If you delete your account (Settings > Delete account), what's personal in it is removed: password, email, avatar, posts, messages and friends. Games and items you made stay up as made by "Deleted user", so people who bought them keep them. Safety records (like bans) can be kept longer to stop people coming back after a ban. Backups roll over and old ones are deleted.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or California law), you can ask to see the data we have about you, fix it, delete it, or take a copy. You can also object to how we use it, or take back your consent for ad cookies (button above). Ask on the Support page, and we'll answer as soon as we can. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Adults only, and children's privacy

Guts&Bolts is for people 18 and older. It is not made for children and isn't aimed at them.

  • We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18, and especially not from children under 13. Everyone who signs up promises they're 18 or older. We don't ask for birthdays or IDs, so we don't keep them.
  • If we find out (or have good reason to think) an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and delete what's personal in it (password, email, avatar, posts, messages and friends) as soon as we can. We don't use what we learned about them for anything else, and we don't send them emails.
  • Parents and guardians: if your child made an account, tell us on the Support page (no account needed). Give their username and we'll close it and delete what's personal in it. We'll only ask for what we need to find the account.
  • Anyone can report an account they think belongs to someone under 18 (pick "Someone under 18").
  • Games can show a content level (Mild to Extreme) so adults know how intense they are. These aren't age ratings: every game here is for adults only.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will also show up on the Updates page.
