Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026. This covers the website (gutsandbolts.net), Studio, the Player and our game servers.

Guts&Bolts is a free hobby game platform for adults (18+). This page says, in plain words, what we keep about you, why, who else sees it, and how to get it removed. The short version: we keep what's needed to run your account, we don't sell your data, and we show ads (from Google) to help pay for the servers.

1. What we keep

  • Your account: username, user number, your public signing key (your password never leaves your device in a readable form), avatar, friends, groups, what you own and make, and your Bolts history.
  • An email address, only if you add one. It's used for account emails, like login codes and resetting your password, and for answers to support tickets.
  • What you post and send: forum posts, comments, messages, chats, games and uploads.
  • Safety records: reports, warnings and bans, so staff can keep the platform safe.
  • Technical info: like your IP address and browser, which our host (Cloudflare) uses to keep the site running and to stop attacks and bots. We keep short logs with web addresses cleaned of private parts.

2. Why we keep it

To run your account and the games, to keep everyone safe (moderation and stopping abuse), to fix problems, and to show ads that pay for the servers. Where the law asks for a reason (like the GDPR in Europe): running the service you signed up for, keeping it safe (our legitimate interest), and your consent for ad cookies.

3. Ads and cookies

Guts&Bolts shows banner ads from Google AdSense on a few pages (like Home, Games and the Catalog). Ads are never shown to Boltworks Club members.

  • Google and its partners use cookies to show ads, measure them, and stop ad fraud. Some ads may be picked based on your past visits to this and other websites.
  • How Google uses information from sites that show its ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can turn off personalized ads from Google at adssettings.google.com, and learn about opting out of other ad companies at aboutads.info.
  • In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask before any ad cookies are used. If you pick "Only necessary", you won't see ads at all.

Our own site only stores what it needs to work: your login key (encrypted, in your browser), your language, a few display choices, and your cookie answer. Those don't track you anywhere else.

Done. You'll be asked again on the next page with ads.

4. Who else handles your data

We don't sell your data to anyone. These services help run Guts&Bolts and only get what they need:

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and servers, and runs the "are you a person?" check.
  • Supabase stores some of the site's data (like leaderboards and activity feeds).
  • Brevo sends account emails, if you added an email address.
  • Google AdSense shows the ads (section 3).

If your uploads are used to help train Fyxa, our built-in AI helper, there's a switch in Settings to turn that off.

5. How long we keep it

For as long as your account is open. If you delete your account (Settings > Delete account), what's personal in it is removed: password, email, avatar, posts, messages and friends. Games and items you made stay up as made by "Deleted user", so people who bought them keep them. Safety records (like bans) can be kept longer to stop people coming back after a ban. Backups roll over and old ones are deleted.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or California law), you can ask to see the data we have about you, fix it, delete it, or take a copy. You can also object to how we use it, or take back your consent for ad cookies (button above). Ask on the Support page, and we'll answer as soon as we can. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Adults only, and children's privacy

Guts&Bolts is for people 18 and older. It is not made for children and isn't aimed at them.

  • We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18, and especially not from children under 13. Everyone who signs up promises they're 18 or older. We don't ask for birthdays or IDs, so we don't keep them.
  • If we find out (or have good reason to think) an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and delete what's personal in it (password, email, avatar, posts, messages and friends) as soon as we can. We don't use what we learned about them for anything else, and we don't send them emails.
  • Parents and guardians: if your child made an account, tell us on the Support page (no account needed). Give their username and we'll close it and delete what's personal in it. We'll only ask for what we need to find the account.
  • Anyone can report an account they think belongs to someone under 18 (pick "Someone under 18").
  • Games can show a content level (Mild to Extreme) so adults know how intense they are. These aren't age ratings: every game here is for adults only.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will also show up on the Updates page.
